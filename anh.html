🧠 CHEAT SHEET TỔNG HỢP

  • To-infinitive: the first/last/only + to V (mục đích).
  • V-ing: sau giới từ. Having + V3: hành động trước.
  • It is/was ... that/who: câu chẻ nhấn mạnh.
  • Because + S+V ≠ Because of + N. Although ≠ In spite of + N.
  • Mệnh đề quan hệ: who (người), which (vật).
  • Cấu trúc: manage to do, accused of having V3, deny having V3, remember having V3, get into the habit of V-ing.
  • Từ vựng theo cụm: make an appointment, manage money, lead to, come up with, make use of, carry out.
  • Conversation: Greeting → Question → Answer → Explain → Opinion.
